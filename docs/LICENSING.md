# Licensing and future paid offerings

ConstellationBar's first-party source is distributed under the MIT license in the repository root. The website source is included under the same license. Third-party components retain their own notices and terms.

MIT allows the maintainer to charge for official builds, support, updates or services, and to develop proprietary additions. Charging does not require a change to the existing open-source license. MIT also permits other people to redistribute, modify and sell copies, provided they retain the required copyright and license notice. This project does not promise that future official downloads or services will always be free of charge.

A paid official build can offer convenience and trust: a signed/notarized download, supported updates and support. The source can remain publicly buildable. MIT does not give the maintainer an exclusive right to sell copies or prevent users from sharing them.

A GPLv3 approach would also allow charging, while requiring corresponding source and license compliance when distributing covered derivatives. It would impose different obligations on proprietary additions and contributions. A noncommercial restriction would not meet the standard open-source definition. Do not silently add one to MIT.

Contributors retain ownership of their contributions and submit them under this repository's MIT license. There is no copyright assignment or exclusive relicensing right. Changes to already-distributed third-party or contributed code must respect the permissions actually granted; do not assume a future license change can revoke existing users' rights.

The license does not imply endorsement of a fork or grant rights to third-party trademarks. Forks should identify their origin accurately and avoid claiming to be an official release. Third-party names and icons in app previews identify integrations; they are not claims of affiliation.
